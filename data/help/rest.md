keywords: [rest, wake, sleeping, sit, stand]
title: Resting and Posture
category: command
min_level: 0
usage: "sit | rest | sleep | stand | wake"
---
Your posture decides how fast you recover. Per second:

  stand     stamina only; hit points do not come back by themselves
  sit       a little hit points, more stamina
  rest      more hit points, more stamina
  sleep     the most of both, but you are deaf and blind to the room

"stand" gets you up and "wake" ends sleep. Being hit while sitting, resting
or asleep hurts more, and you cannot recover while you fight. You may still
fight from any posture; the game does not stand you up.

Meditating (MEDITATE) shortens spell slot recovery for casters.

See also: MEDITATE, SLOTS, FLEE.
