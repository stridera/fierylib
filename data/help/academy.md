The Ethilien Training Academy is the school for new characters. It
teaches the basics: moving, talking, fighting, gear and exploring. Every
new player is welcome, whatever race you picked and wherever you woke up.

FINDING IT
  The entrance is below the Forest Temple of Mielikki, in the Village of
  Mielikki (zone 30). The temple stands just north of Town Center. From
  the temple, type "down".
  Humans start inside the temple, so for them it is one word away. Other
  races start in their own homelands and need to walk to the village
  first. "areas" lists the zones; "where" tells you what room you are in.

AT THE ACADEMY
  A Recruiter waits at the gates. Talk to a teacher with "say": a
  teacher answers the words it listens for, and tells you the next one.
  You can leave at any time by saying "exit".

AFTER THE ACADEMY
  The Mielikki Farmlands (zone 80) is the newbie zone: easy monsters and
  safe fighting. "help newbie" has the rest of the first steps.

See also: NEWBIE.
