Shops are run by shopkeeper NPCs. Stand in the same room as the keeper.

  list              show what the keeper sells: item number, name, price
                    and stock ("unlimited" or a count)
  buy 3             buy item number 3 from the list
  buy dagger        buy by a word from the item's name
  sell sword        sell something you are carrying
  value sword       show an item's base value, weight and minimum level
                    (it searches your inventory and the room)

  Coins come out of, and go into, the coin you carry (see "wealth").
  Anything you can't afford is refused with the amount you are short.
  Keepers pay only a fraction of an item's base value, refuse items
  they don't deal in and anything worthless, and won't buy soulbound
  items. Selling works on what you carry, so remove worn gear first.

PET SHOPS
  Some keepers also have pets for hire. "list" shows them in a second
  table. "hire {#|name}" buys one; it becomes your follower and is named
  after you, for example "Strider's pony".

REPAIRS
  "repair {item}" asks a shopkeeper to refill a lantern or torch for
  10 copper per missing game-hour of fuel. Other items don't wear out.

COINS
  10 copper = 1 silver, 10 silver = 1 gold, 10 gold = 1 platinum.
  Prices are shown broken into those coins.
