title: Mortal mode

MORTAL switches you between playing as an immortal and playing under mortal
rules, so you can retest what players experience. Type it once to turn mortal
mode on and again to turn it off. While it is on your prompt shows <mortal>.

While mortal mode is on:
  - spells use your spell slots and recover over time like anyone else's
  - you must know a spell or skill, and meet its class and alignment limits,
    to use it
  - gear restrictions (class, race, alignment, no-take) apply to you
  - carry weight and capacity limits apply
  - spells have a cast wind-up and you are held back after a broken-off cast
  - spells that cannot be cast in combat are refused while you fight

Your rank, commands and permissions do not change: goto, stat, users and every
other staff command still work. Mortal mode is not saved, so you start each
login with it off.

See also: HOLYLIGHT, SPELLS, SCORE
