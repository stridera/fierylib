Your class gives you abilities; your proficiency decides how well you use
them. Abilities are spells, skills, chants and songs.

LISTS
  skills             skills you know       skills all   every skill
  spells / chants / songs   the same, for the other kinds
  innate             abilities your race gives you
  practice           everything you know, with proficiency shown as a
                     percentage and a rank: novice, apprentice, skilled,
                     expert, master. A star marks one you are still
                     learning. It also shows your practice points and, for
                     most abilities, the highest your class allows.

PRACTICE POINTS
  You earn practice points by gaining levels. Spend them with a trainer
  (a trainer or guildmaster NPC in the room):

    practice fireball    spend 1 point: proficiency goes up by 5, up to
                         your class's limit for that ability
    train                list your six stats and your points
    train str            spend 1 point to raise strength by 1

  Abilities have to be learned first ("study {spell}" for spells). If
  practice says an ability isn't on your class's list, your class can't
  improve it this way. Stats stop rising at 18, or at your race's own
  maximum if that is lower. The stats are str, dex, con, int, wis, cha.

  Listing with no argument works anywhere; spending a point needs a
  trainer.

Skills you know are also used by name, like kick or backstab; "help
{skill}" explains each one.

See also: MAGIC, NEWBIE.
