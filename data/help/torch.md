title: Torches and Light
category: command
min_level: 0
usage: "light {torch} | extinguish {torch}"
---
At night and underground the world is dark: you cannot see the room, the
exits or other characters. Some races see in the dark; most need a light.

  light {torch}        turn a carried light on
  extinguish {torch}   turn it off to save its fuel

A torch burns for a while and then goes out. General stores sell torches
and lanterns: Aaron's General Store in the Village of Mielikki (zone 30)
is one. Buy one before you go out after dark.

See also: LIGHT, SHOPS, NEWBIE.
