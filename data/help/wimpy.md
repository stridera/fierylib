---
keywords: [wimpy]
title: Wimpy
category: command
min_level: 0
usage: "wimpy [{percent}|off]"
---
WIMPY makes you flee a fight by yourself when your hit points drop below
a percentage of your maximum.

  wimpy 30         flee when below 30 percent
  wimpy off        turn it off (wimpy 0 does the same)
  wimpy            show the current setting

New characters start with wimpy on, so a losing fight ends in a flee and
not a death.

See also: FLEE.
