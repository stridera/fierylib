Welcome to FieryMUD. These are the first steps for a new character. Type
"help {topic}" for details on anything named here.

LOOK AROUND
  look               describe the room         exits    list the exits
  north (n), south (s), east (e), west (w), up (u), down (d) move you.
  scan               peek at the rooms next to you.
  score              your stats       inventory (i)    what you carry
  equipment (eq)     what you wear    wealth           your coin

GEAR UP
  wear {item}, wield {weapon}, remove {item}, get, drop, give.
  A badly dressed character takes far more damage. Shops sell gear:
  see SHOPS.

LEARN YOUR CLASS
  skills, spells and practice show what you can do. Trainers (NPCs) teach
  you and spend the practice points you earn by levelling. See SKILL and
  MAGIC. "where {name}" can help you find someone.

FIGHT SMART
  consider {target}  tells you how dangerous something is. Try it first.
  kill {target}      start a fight.          flee    run away.
  wimpy 30           flee automatically below 30 percent HP.
  rest, sleep and food and drink recover HP and stamina; "stand" gets you
  up. Check hunger and thirst on score.

STAY SAFE
  Touch a touchstone in a safe place, and "recall" will take you back to
  it from anywhere. Keep spare coin in the BANK.

PLAY WITH OTHERS
  GROUPS, CHANNELS (gossip, tell), CLANS, BOARDS and MAIL. Quests are
  listed with "quests"; see QUESTS.

SETTINGS AND ACCOUNT
  PROMPT, color, brief, alias. For passwords, the website and the
  "code" login, see WEBSITE.

Lost? "commands" lists every command, and "help" with no topic shows the
index.
