min_level: 0

---
Hunger and thirst show on SCORE. They do not slow healing, but they do
wear you down over time, and a full stomach is a good habit.

  eat {food}           eat bread, rations and the like
  drink {container}    drink from a waterskin, cup or flask you carry
  drink {fountain}     drink from a fountain in the room

Fountains in towns are free. Inns and general stores sell food and
drink. Torches and lanterns are sold there too: see TORCH.

See also: EAT, HUNGER, SHOPS.
