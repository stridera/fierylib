---
keywords: [where]
title: Where
category: command
min_level: 0
usage: "where [{player}|corpse]"
---
WHERE tells you where something is.

  where              the room you are standing in, with its [zone:id]
  where {player}     the room of a player who is online
  where corpse       where your own corpse lies, and which way to go

Monsters and items cannot be located this way. To find a monster, walk
the zone, or use TRACK if your class has it. "areas" lists the zones.

See also: AREAS, TRACK, DEATH.
