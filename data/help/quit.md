keywords: [quit]
title: Quit
min_level: 0
---
When you get tired of playing, type QUIT to leave the game. Your character is
saved where you stand: your equipment, items and money stay with you, and
when you enter the game again you come back to the same room.

Resting at an inn (RENT) is the same, except you return to the inn's
reception room.

See also: RENT, INN, CAMP
