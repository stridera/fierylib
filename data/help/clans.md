A clan is a player organisation with its own roster, message of the day and
private chat channel. Clans are created and staffed by the immortals: you
join one when staff assign you to it, and they set your rank (leader,
officer or member). If you want to found or join a clan, ask a god.

COMMANDS
  clan               show your clan's name, abbreviation, message of the
                     day and roster. The roster is sorted leader, officer,
                     member, then by level and name; an asterisk marks
                     members who are online.
  ctell {message}    talk on the clan channel (also "ct"). Every online
                     member hears it.
  clan motd {text}   set the clan's message of the day. Leaders and
                     officers only. "clan motd" with no text clears it.
  clan kick {name}   remove a member from the clan. Leaders only.

  If you aren't in a clan, these commands tell you so.

See also: CHANNELS, GROUPS.
