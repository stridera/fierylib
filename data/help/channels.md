Ways to talk, from closest to furthest.

IN THE ROOM
  say hello there          speak to the room (also ')
  whisper Aria meet me     only Aria hears the words; others see you whisper
  ask {mob} {topic}        ask an NPC about something (starts quests)
  emote waves hello        everyone sees "Strider waves hello." (also :)
  socials                  list the premade social commands (smile, bow...)

PRIVATE
  tell Aria hello          message one player anywhere (also t)
  reply thanks             answer whoever tell'd you last (also r)
  lasttells                your recent tells

GROUP AND CLAN
  gsay {message}           your group     (also gtell, gt)
  ctell {message}          your clan      (also ct)

WORLD CHANNELS
  gossip {message}         everyone online (also / and .). Hints about the
                           world are welcome; please keep quest answers out.
  shout {message}          to everyone (also sh)
  music {message}          song lyrics and similar
  qsay {message}           the quest channel, for questers to coordinate
  lastgossips [n]  lastshouts [n]    replay recent lines (up to 50)

CONTROLS
  deaf                     stop hearing gossip and shout; type again to hear
  notell                   refuse tells; type again to allow them
  afk                      mark yourself away; type again to return
  ignore Bob               block Bob's tells and channel messages
  ignore -Bob              stop ignoring Bob (or: unignore Bob)
  ignore clear             empty the list
  The ignore list lasts for the current session only.

See also: GROUPS, CLANS, MAIL, BOARDS.
