Casters learn their spells with STUDY. Skills come with your class as you
level and need no study.

  study              list the spells you can learn right now
  study {spell}      learn one

A spell is listed once you have reached its circle, and not before. Quest
spells are not offered. Learn one, then "spells" shows what you know and
"practice" shows how well.

Spell slots are separate. "slots" shows how many you have ready and how
long the spent ones take to recover. Slots recover on their own, faster
while you rest or meditate, so there is nothing to memorize.

See also: SPELLS, SLOTS, MAGIC, MEDITATE, PRACTICE.
